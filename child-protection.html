Youth Safety Protection Framework

1. Core Commitment to Adolescent Online Well‑being

Ovia – Real Voices Connections is dedicated to fostering an encouraging, safe, and transparent online community for all individuals utilizing our platform. We place the highest priority on protecting minors, teenagers, and under-aged users from online exploitation, abuse, and inappropriate interactions.

To ensure a secure environment, we continuously enhance our youth-focused safety mechanisms, automated content filtering systems, and manual content moderation workflows. Our goal is to provide a voice-driven social service where young people can interact within an accountable, secure, and wholesome digital setting.

This framework outlines the comprehensive, end‑to‑end safeguards enforced by Ovia – Real Voices Connections to protect young users. These measures strictly cover:

  • Strict user age verification and identity validation protocols.
  • Proactive user-generated content (UGC) oversight, automated keyword screening, and audio reporting benchmarks.
  • Comprehensive parental controls and guardian-managed administrative features.
  • Standard operating procedures for immediate response to urgent safety incidents and policy violations.
  • Full compliance with global regulatory standards and Google Play Developer Policies regarding child safety.

2. Age Validation & Platform Eligibility Criteria

To prevent minors from accessing age-restricted features or mature content, Ovia – Real Voices Connections enforces strict age gates and multi-layered identity validation measures across all services.

Eligibility Rules Based On User Age

  • Minimum Age Requirement: Users must be at least 13 years old to register an account and utilize basic platform services.
  • Adult-Only Features: Advanced functionalities, including mature communication zones and unrestricted interactions, remain accessible solely to verified individuals aged 18 or older.
  • Guardian Consent: Users under the age of 18 must obtain explicit consent from their parents or legal guardians prior to accessing the application, in compliance with regional minor protection regulations.

Multi-Level Age Verification & Identity Confirmation Procedures

  • Mandatory Date-of-Birth Input: A complete and valid date of birth must be supplied during the initial account creation workflow to calculate user age dynamically.
  • Real-Time Age Gates: Instant verification checks trigger automatically whenever a user attempts to access any restricted content or localized age-sensitive features.
  • Identity Verification for UGC Publishing: Official identity or parental verification is required for minor users before they are permitted to broadcast public audio recordings or publish media files.
  • Automated and Manual Account Audits: Continuous account reviews and behavioral analysis are executed by our moderation team to detect, flag, and suspend profiles containing fraudulent age or profile data.

3. Content Governance & Prohibited Content Moderation

Ovia – Real Voices Connections enforces a comprehensive User-Generated Content (UGC) policy backed by automated filtering and active human oversight. We maintain an absolute zero-tolerance stance toward any material that threatens the physical, mental, or emotional safety of minors and adolescents.

Automated Content Moderation & Screening Filters

  • Real-Time UGC Inspection: Automated screening filters review and block non-compliant text, imagery, and audio recordings immediately upon submission.
  • Dynamic Keyword Filtering: Real-time detection systems intercept hate speech, aggressive harassment, bullying, and abusive verbal or text communications.
  • Audio and Visual Monitoring: AI-powered analysis engines systematically scan multimedia elements to flag content unsuitable for minors based on standardized age ratings.

24/7 Human Review & Incident Escalation Team

  • Dedicated Safety Operations: A specialized trust and safety unit works around the clock (24/7/365) to evaluate flagged material and user-submitted reports.
  • Certified Review Training: Every human moderator undergoes mandatory, standardized training focused on child online protection, safety assessment, and rapid incident handling.
  • Expedited Minor Reporting Queues: Any content report or infraction that impacts or references a minor user bypasses general backlogs and is automatically prioritized for immediate action.

Content Classification & App Environment Controls

  • Age-Appropriate Architecture: A unified content categorization framework dictates what interactions, media types, and chat groups are visible to different user age brackets.
  • Restricted Access Gates: Complete identity and age verification is required before any user is allowed access to unrestricted or mature interactive channels.

Immediate Removals & Account Sanctions:
Any user submissions containing or promoting violent imagery, sexually explicit material, cyber-bullying, harassment, or self-harm/suicide guidance are completely prohibited. Content of this nature is deleted permanently without warning, and the offending accounts face immediate, irreversible device and IP address suspension.

Strict Prohibition of CSAE

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy regarding child exploitation and strictly prohibit Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and CSAE in alignment with Google Play Child Safety Standards. Violations result in permanent bans and reporting to NCMEC. Contact: raheelk.dev@gmail.com.
