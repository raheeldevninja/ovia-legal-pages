1. Child Safety Standards & Zero-Tolerance CSAE/CSAM Policy

Published Standards in Compliance with Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy

Developer Declaration by raheel khan for Ovia – Real Voices Connections:

Ovia – Real Voices Connections maintains a strict ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICY against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), child exploitation, child grooming, and any action that endangers the safety, well-being, or privacy of minors.

A. Explicit Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

Users of Ovia – Real Voices Connections are strictly prohibited from generating, transmitting, hosting, sharing, storing, soliciting, or displaying any content related to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form (visual, text, audio stream, or link).
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), including child sexual solicitation, sextortion, or trafficking.
  • Child grooming behavior, illegal contact with minors, or sexualization of children under 18 years of age.
  • Any communication, live voice interaction, or user profile content that facilitates child endangerment.

B. Moderation, Reporting & Immediate Enforcement Actions

To enforce these safety standards, developer raheel khan operates multi-layered safety mechanisms within Ovia – Real Voices Connections:

In-App User Reporting

Users can report any inappropriate room, profile, voice chat, or text message instantly using in-app report buttons. All CSAE/CSAM reports are immediately flagged for priority urgent review.

Immediate Account Termination

Any account found engaging in CSAE/CSAM activity is permanently banned immediately, content is deleted within 24 hours, and hardware/device access is blocked permanently.

C. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC

In compliance with international laws and Google Play Child Safety Policies, developer raheel khan immediately reports any identified CSAM or CSAE content and associated user details to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and relevant local/international law enforcement authorities.

3. Information We Collect

To operate Ovia – Real Voices Connections and enable real-time voice interactions, we collect the following categories of personal and technical data:

A. Account & Profile Information

When you register an account on Ovia – Real Voices Connections, we collect your self-provided display name, profile picture/avatar, user bio, email address, and account login identifiers.

B. Voice & Audio Data

Ovia – Real Voices Connections is a voice-focused social networking app. Live voice audio streams are processed in real-time to facilitate group audio rooms and direct voice calls. Live voice streams are processed ephemerally for real-time transmission and are not permanently recorded or saved unless explicitly saved by user actions or flagged for moderation/safety reviews.

C. Technical & Device Identifiers

We automatically collect hardware device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID), IP address, app version, network connectivity status, crash reports, and system performance logs to maintain app security and stability.

D. User-Generated Content & Communications

Text messages, images, audio clips, feedback submissions, and content shared inside public or private rooms within Ovia – Real Voices Connections.

4. Microphone Access & Device Permissions Declaration

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies on App Permissions and User Data:

Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Ovia – Real Voices Connections requires access to your device microphone solely to enable core features such as live voice room participation, real-time voice streaming, and voice messages. Microphone data is accessed strictly while you actively use voice features and is never accessed secretly or in the background without user context.

5. Purposes of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Providing and delivering core real-time voice communication features of Ovia – Real Voices Connections.
  • Authenticating user accounts and safeguarding user profiles from unauthorized access.
  • Enforcing Child Safety Standards, community guidelines, preventing harassment, and removing prohibited CSAE/CSAM content.
  • Diagnosing server crashes, technical bugs, and optimizing audio stream stability.
  • Responding to user customer support requests and data privacy inquiries.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

Developer raheel khan DOES NOT SELL your personal data to third parties or data brokers. Data disclosure occurs only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Infrastructure Providers: Third-party cloud hosting, real-time voice infrastructure (RTC SDKs), and analytics services operating under strict data confidentiality agreements.
  • Law Enforcement & NCMEC: Disclosure when mandated by law, court order, or when necessary to protect child safety, report CSAE/CSAM, or prevent imminent harm.
  • Protection of Rights: Enforcing our Terms of Service and defending user safety against malicious abuse or fraud.
