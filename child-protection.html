1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against CSAE & CSAM

Developer Commitment by raheel khan:

Ovia – Real Voices Connections enforces a strict ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICY prohibiting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), child grooming, or any form of child endangerment.

A. Explicitly Prohibited Activities

Users of Ovia – Real Voices Connections are strictly forbidden from producing, sharing, soliciting, facilitating, or linking to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) across text, audio streams, images, or shared links.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), including child sexual solicitation, sextortion, or trafficking.
  • Child grooming activities, deceptive interactions targeting minors, or sexualization of individuals under 18.
  • Any live voice room interaction or profile content that poses a threat to minor safety.

B. Strict Enforcement Actions

Any user account associated with CSAE/CSAM violations triggers immediate enforcement:

Permanent Account Ban Immediate termination of profile access and permanent hardware/IP device block.
Content Deletion All associated content, messages, and uploaded assets are purged within 24 hours.
Zero Appeal for CSAE Violations involving minor exploitation carry permanent non-appealable bans.

2. Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Escalation

Developer raheel khan complies fully with statutory reporting obligations under international minor protection regulations and Google Play Developer Policies.

Reporting Procedure for Confirmed or Suspected CSAE/CSAM:

  • Immediate notification and evidence submission to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.
  • Direct cooperation with local and international law enforcement agencies by preserving necessary audit logs and user details for legal prosecution.

3. Content Governance & Safety Reporting Mechanisms

To maintain a secure environment in Ovia – Real Voices Connections, safety workflows operate continuously:

Direct Safety & Incident Reporting

Users and the public can report any inappropriate content, voice interaction, user profile, or child safety concern directly to our safety team via email (raheelk.dev@gmail.com). All safety and minor protection reports are prioritized for immediate review and swift action.

Proactive Moderation

Combination of automated safety filters, keyword scanning, and human moderation teams reviewing flagged content to prevent abusive behavior and ensure a safe platform.

4. Age Eligibility & Access Restrictions

  • Minimum Age Requirement (18+ Only): Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use Ovia – Real Voices Connections. Minors (anyone under 18 years of age) are strictly prohibited from creating accounts or accessing the app.
  • Age Verification & Age Gates: Account registration enforces date-of-birth validation and age gates to block anyone under 18 from registering.
  • Immediate Removal of Underage Accounts: If we discover or are notified that an account belongs to a minor under 18, the account will be immediately terminated and permanently deleted. Parents, guardians, or users can report unauthorized minor accounts to raheelk.dev@gmail.com for immediate action.
