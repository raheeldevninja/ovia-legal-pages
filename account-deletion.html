Delete your Ovia account

Use the steps below to permanently delete your account and associated data from Ovia – Real Voices Connections.

⚠️

Important: Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, posts, messages, connections, activity history, preferences, and other account-associated data, subject to limited retention required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. This action cannot be undone.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open the Ovia app and sign in to your account.

  2. Open Settings by tapping the gear icon or your profile menu.

  3. Scroll to Delete Account, tap it, review the information, and select Confirm to complete the deletion request.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or the deletion option is unavailable, contact the Ovia support team. Include the email address or username associated with your account so we can verify and assist with your request.
